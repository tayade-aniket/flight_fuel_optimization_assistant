Key Findings

⏱ Duration Dominates

Interval duration is by far the strongest predictor — longer intervals accumulate more fuel burn regardless of other factors.

⚖️ Mass Matters

Aircraft reference mass captures the physics of drag — heavier airframes burn proportionally more per interval.

🏔 High FL = Less Drag

Flight level has a negative SHAP contribution — higher cruise altitudes reduce aerodynamic drag, lowering fuel burn at cruise speeds.

📐 Distance + Speed

Distance flown and groundspeed jointly capture the work done against drag — both correlate positively with fuel burn.

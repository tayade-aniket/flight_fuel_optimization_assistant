System Architecture & Core Capabilities

AeroFuel AI bridges raw aviation big data, machine learning, and airline flight operations control:

  1. ACARS + ADS-B Telemetry Fusion — fuses sub-second kinematic trajectory tracks (OpenSky Network) with ground-truth fuel-on-board telemetry (airframes.io) from the official EUROCONTROL PRC 2025 Data Challenge.
  2. Trajectory-Aware Modeling — extracts 3D kinematics (rate of climb/descent, flight path curvature, altitude levels, groundspeed) rather than simplistic O–D distance.
  3. Physics-Residual Hybrid ML — combines aircraft energy-balance physics baseline with XGBoost/LightGBM residual booster to guarantee energy conservation.
  4. Explainable AI (SHAP) — clarifies the specific operational drivers behind fuel consumption without implying false physical causation.
  5. Constrained What-If Optimizer — solves for optimal cruise FL and speed within RVSM envelopes subject to schedule delay penalties and aircraft service ceilings.
